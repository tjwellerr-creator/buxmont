Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company based in Bucks County, serving Bucks County, Montgomery County, and Philadelphia. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Do I need a permit for an addition or sunroom?

Yes. Additions and sunrooms almost always need a building permit, and many need a zoning review for setbacks. We prepare the drawings and handle the approvals.

What's the difference between a sunroom and an addition?

A sunroom is built around windows and light, while a traditional addition has standard walls and is usually designed as full living space. We'll walk you through the options based on how you plan to use the room.

Will the addition match my house?

That's the goal from the first drawing. We plan rooflines, siding, windows, and floor heights so the new space looks like part of the original home.

Can we stay home during construction?

In most cases, yes. We keep the work area organized and clean up daily so you can keep living in the house.
