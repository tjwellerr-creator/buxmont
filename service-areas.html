Where we work

Permits work differently in every town

Part of what we do is know who approves your project and what they need to see. Here’s how it works in each area we serve.

Home base

Bucks County

Bucks County is home base. Each township and borough here runs its own building permits and inspections, often through a third-party code agency. We check what your municipality requires, prepare the drawings to match, and handle the submission.

Next door

Montgomery County

We work throughout Montgomery County too. Permits are handled township by township, just like in Bucks, so we confirm the requirements for your address before the drawings are finalized.

In the city

Philadelphia

In Philadelphia, permits and plan review go through the Department of Licenses and Inspections (L&I), and many projects also need a zoning permit. We prepare the drawings and handle the application for you.
