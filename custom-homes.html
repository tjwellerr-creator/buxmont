Custom Homes

A new home, planned and built by the same people

Building a house usually means hiring an architect, a drafter, a permit runner, and a builder, then hoping they stay on the same page. Yeshua draws the plans in-house and manages the build, so the people who design your home are the same people responsible for building it.

We build new homes in Philadelphia and across the surrounding suburbs, and we bring in licensed architects and structural engineers whenever a project calls for stamped drawings.

  • Design and drafting. Floor plans and elevations drawn in-house, with architects and engineers coordinated as needed.
  • Site work. Site planning, excavation, and foundation.
  • The full build. Framing, roofing, siding, and complete interior finishes.
  • Approvals. Permits, zoning, and township or Philadelphia L&I approvals handled for you.

Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs in Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware, and Chester Counties. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Do you design the house, or do I need an architect?

We draft plans in-house and bring in a licensed architect or structural engineer when the project or the municipality requires stamped drawings. Either way, you work with one team.

Can you build on a lot I already own?

Yes. We visit the lot first to look at access, utilities, slope, and zoning before we start on plans.

How long does a custom home take?

It depends on the size and design of the home and how long approvals take where you're building. You'll get a realistic schedule with your estimate and regular updates once work starts.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and handle permits, zoning, and inspections with Philadelphia L&I or your township.
