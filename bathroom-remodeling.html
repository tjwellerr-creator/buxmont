Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs in Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware, and Chester Counties. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Do I need a permit to remodel a bathroom?

Remodels that move plumbing, add electrical, or change walls usually do. We'll tell you what your project needs and handle it.

Can you build a curbless walk-in shower?

Yes. Curbless and walk-in showers are planned with the floor slope and waterproofing built in from the start.

How long will we be without the bathroom?

It depends on the scope of the remodel. You'll get a schedule with your estimate so you can plan around it.
