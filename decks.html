Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company based in Bucks County, serving Bucks County, Montgomery County, and Philadelphia. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Do I need a permit to build a deck?

Usually, yes. Most townships in Bucks and Montgomery County, and Philadelphia, require a permit for decks that attach to the house or sit above a certain height. We draw the plans and handle the permit.

Composite or wood: which should I choose?

Composite costs more up front but needs much less upkeep, with no staining or sealing. Pressure-treated wood costs less to build but needs regular maintenance. We can price both so you can compare.

Can I see the design before you build?

Yes. We can provide a 3D layout so you can see the size, shape, and railings before construction starts.
