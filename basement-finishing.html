Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company based in Bucks County, serving Bucks County, Montgomery County, and Philadelphia. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Do I need a permit to finish my basement?

Generally, yes, especially when the work includes new walls, electrical, plumbing, or a bedroom. We handle the permit and inspections.

What if my basement gets damp?

We check for moisture first. If waterproofing is needed, it gets handled before any framing goes up.

Can you add a bathroom in the basement?

Yes. Basement bathrooms and wet bars are a common part of our basement projects.

Do I need an egress window?

If the basement will include a bedroom, code requires an emergency escape opening such as an egress window. We'll tell you exactly what applies to your plan.
