Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Yeshua Drafting and Construction Management (“Yeshua,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you send an estimate request, we receive:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, and preferred way to be contacted
  • The project address (street, town, state, and ZIP)
  • Details about your project, such as project type, property type, budget range, timeline, and your description
  • How you heard about us, if you tell us

If you call, text, or email us directly, we receive whatever you include in that message.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and it does not collect information about you just for visiting.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule a call or site visit
  • To prepare an estimate and, if you hire us, to plan and carry out your project
  • To keep records of our communication and work with you

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party marketing.

Service providers

Estimate requests sent through this website are delivered to our email by FormSubmit, a form-delivery service, and are then stored in our email account. These providers handle your information only to deliver and store our messages. Our website host may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

We may also share information when required by law, or with subcontractors and suppliers only as needed to complete a project you have hired us for.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, complete and support your project, and meet our business, warranty, and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we have about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time. Just email or call us using the details below.

Security

This website is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive information such as financial or account numbers in the form.

Children

This website is meant for adults planning construction projects. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
