Why does the base matter so much?

Pavers are only as flat as what's under them. A properly excavated, graded, and compacted base is what keeps a patio from sinking or holding water.

Do I need a permit for a patio?

Often not for a simple ground-level patio, but retaining walls, outdoor kitchens with gas or electric, and work that changes drainage can need approval. We'll check what your municipality requires.

Can you add a fire pit or seating wall?

Yes. Fire pits, seating walls, and outdoor kitchens can be planned into the same project.
