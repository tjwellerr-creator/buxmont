Drafting & Permit Drawings

Drawings made by people who build

Need plans for a permit but not a full design firm? We draft floor plans, elevations, site layouts, and framing plans for decks, additions, renovations, and new homes, ready to submit to Philadelphia L&I or your township.

Because we build too, our drawings are made with the job site in mind. Hire us for drawings only, or have us take the project all the way through construction.

  • Plans. Floor plans, elevations, and site layouts.
  • Permit sets. Permit-ready drawing sets for township or city submission.
  • Framing plans. Deck, addition, and renovation framing plans.
  • Revisions. 3D layouts and design revisions.

Questions

Common questions

Yeshua is a drafting and construction company serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs in Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware, and Chester Counties. We draw it, permit it, and build it. Licensed PA Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #PA235939.

Can I hire you just for drawings?

Yes. Many clients hire us for drawings only. You can also have us manage or build the project later.

Are your drawings stamped?

Many residential projects don't need stamped drawings. When the project or the municipality requires an architect's or engineer's stamp, we coordinate with licensed professionals.

What do I need to get started?

Any existing plans, a property survey or plot plan if you have one, photos, and an idea of what you want. We'll measure the rest on site.
